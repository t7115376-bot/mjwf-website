§ 03 Recognition

External recognition.

Award

Junoon Award

by Goonj · May 2026

MurariJyoti Welfare Foundation was selected as a Junoon Awardee by Goonj — recognised as a grassroots organisation working in health, mental health, and hygiene in rural Bihar.

The award was received in New Delhi at Goonj's Menstruation Dialogue 2026 special edition. Tejaswini also spoke as a panelist at the event.

May 2026 · New Delhi

About Goonj

Goonj is one of India's most recognised non-profits, known for dignity-driven disaster relief and community development. The Junoon Award recognises grassroots organisations with impact at scale.

Menstruation Dialogue 2026

An annual forum bringing together practitioners, advocates, and grassroots organisations working on menstrual health across India. Tejaswini participated as a speaker alongside other awardees.
